Mapori – Privacy Policy

Updated October 2026

The short version

Mapori collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer cannot see your places.

Where your map lives

The countries, regions, cities, dates and notes you add are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for Mapori, they are also kept in your private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read. They are never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Photos

If you choose “Fill from my photos”, Mapori reads only the location and date of your photos, on your device, to suggest countries and cities. It never reads, copies or uploads the pictures themselves. You review every suggestion before anything is added. Memories on a country’s page show your own photos from there straight from your library; nothing is copied or uploaded. If you use “Choose photos instead”, only the photos you pick are read, in the same way. The explored map shows where your photos were taken, worked out on your device; the background map comes from Apple Maps, which only receives the map area you are looking at. A travel film can include your own photos; it is made on your iPhone and goes only where you share or save it.

Purchases

Any purchase in Mapori, such as Mapori Pro, is handled by Apple through the App Store. Mapori only receives a confirmation that you own it; it never sees your payment details.

Sharing

Share cards are images created on your device. They contain only numbers, country names, months and the globe. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it. A “Compare with a friend” link contains only the codes of the countries you’ve been to and lived in, and your first name if you add one. The list travels inside the link itself (after the #), so it never reaches a server; the web page only opens Mapori.

Location

Mapori does not use your location. Sunset mode estimates sunrise and sunset from your device’s time zone.

Your control

You can export everything as JSON or CSV at any time, and Settings › Delete all data removes everything from this device and from iCloud. “On this day” reminders are optional and scheduled on your iPhone only.
