Frequently asked questions

Does Mapori need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no analytics and no tracking. Your map stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud database.

How do I get my map on my iPad too?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices and make sure iCloud is on for Mapori in Settings › your name › iCloud. Your places sync automatically.

How does “Fill from my photos” work?

Mapori reads only the location and date of your photos, on your device, and suggests the countries and cities you visited. You review every suggestion before anything is added. Later scans only look at photos added since your last scan; tap Scan all photos again to go through your whole library.

Can I back up or move my data?

Yes. Settings › Export (JSON or CSV) saves everything to a file, and Settings › Import from file brings it back on any device.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › Delete all data removes your map from this device and from iCloud. You have 10 seconds to undo.
